Privacy Policy – Science Questions Poster

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Science Questions Poster is a private tool used only by its owner to publish our own short educational videos (science quizzes) to our own TikTok account, @sciencequestions.daily. It is not offered to the public and no other users sign in to it.

What data the tool uses

  • The access token TikTok issues when the account owner authorises the tool, used only to upload and publish videos to that same account.
  • Basic account information returned by TikTok (display name, username, posting settings) needed to publish correctly.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell or share personal data of TikTok users or viewers.
  • We do not read messages, comments, followers or any other user's data.
  • We do not use the data for advertising or profiling.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and are never shared. The owner can revoke access at any time in the TikTok app under Settings › Security › Apps and services.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the TikTok account @sciencequestions.daily.
